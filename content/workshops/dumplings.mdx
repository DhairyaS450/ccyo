Dumplings are the group activity. One person rolls, two people fold, someone insists their pleats are better, and forty dumplings appear in half an hour. This is the version we plan to make together when Lunar New Year comes around in February, written out so you can practise the fold before then.

The filling is the everyday northern classic, pork and garlic chives (韭菜). If you cannot find garlic chives, use napa cabbage: chop it fine, salt it, wait ten minutes, and squeeze out the water before mixing it in.

## 皮 Wrappers

Store-bought wrappers work, and nobody will judge you. Homemade ones are softer and thicker in the middle, which is where the filling sits, and thinner at the edge, which is where you pleat. The trick is rolling with one hand while the other hand turns the wrapper a quarter turn after every push. Do fifteen and you will have it.

## 褶 The pleat

There are a dozen dumpling folds. Learn one. The half-moon with pleats on one side is the fold most families use and it stands up on its own on the plate.

1. Fold the wrapper over the filling and pinch the very top closed.
2. Hold the dumpling in your left hand with the flat back edge against your thumb.
3. With your right thumb and finger, push a small pleat toward the centre and press it flat against the back edge. Repeat twice more, moving outward.
4. Turn the dumpling and do three pleats on the other side, mirroring the first.
5. Pinch the ends firmly. A leaky corner will burst in the water.

<Callout title="Potstickers">
Same dumplings, different pan. Heat a tablespoon of oil in a non-stick pan, arrange the dumplings flat side down, fry for 2 minutes until golden, then add half a cup of water and cover. Steam 6 minutes, uncover, and cook until the water is gone and the bottoms are crisp.
</Callout>

## 问题 Questions

<Q q="Can I freeze them?">
Yes, and you should. Freeze the uncooked dumplings on a tray until hard, then bag them. Cook from frozen, adding two minutes.
</Q>

<Q q="Why do mine fall apart in the pot?">
Either the edges had flour on them when you sealed them, or the pot was boiling too hard. Wipe the flour off the edge with a wet finger before folding, and keep the water at a steady boil rather than a rolling one.
</Q>

<Q q="Vegetarian?">
Swap the pork for 300 g of firm tofu, crumbled and squeezed dry, plus a handful of chopped shiitake mushrooms fried until browned. Season the same way.
</Q>
